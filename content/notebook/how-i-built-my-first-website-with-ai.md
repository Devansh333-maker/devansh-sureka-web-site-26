(Written about the first version of this site, in 2025.)

Curious how I built my website? I built it with AI, and it took 30 days.

I tried plenty of AI website builders and templates, but none of them fit what I wanted. Then someone I know suggested Firebase AI, and that’s when things clicked. I created the whole website with AI: gave it plenty of prompts, explored different web designs, and combined all my work into documents, words and animations, so the site felt like mine and showed everything I’ve done.

And finally, it was done.

> If you really want your own flavour, your own style, then trust me, it’s not easy. It’s difficult.

To the people who say building a website from scratch with AI is easy: if there’s an error, you find it, tell the AI, it solves it… and sometimes the problem still isn’t fixed. So you try again. And again. That cycle is how real creation happens.

This was my first blog post. More are coming.
